Beet is a free, open-source GitHub dashboard that lives in your macOS menu bar. It watches the pull requests and CI runs you actually have a stake in, so one glance answers the only question that matters: what needs me right now?

  • Runs entirely on your Mac
  • No server, no account, no telemetry

Three things it does

01

A queue that ranks itself

Every review request gets scored. PRs from people on your team and requests aimed straight at you rise to the top; drafts, stale PRs, and anything you already approved sink or drop off entirely.

The top of the list is your answer. No triage tax.

02

Lives in the menu bar

A beet in your menu bar with a badge count of unread items that actually need you. Click for the popover, open the full window when you're ready to triage.

Closing the window doesn't quit it — the tray keeps watching until you say otherwise.

03

Updates in the background

Polling adapts on its own: in-flight work refreshes fast, everything else slow, and intervals back off when the window is hidden, you're on battery, or the rate limit gets tight.

Notifications stay on a tight budget — merge-queue ejections, failing checks, and @mentions. Everything else updates silently.

Running in three steps

  1. Install it

    Download the .dmg and drag Beet to Applications. The build isn't signed yet, so right-click the app and choose Open the first time.

  2. Paste a token

    Beet authenticates with a classic personal access token you paste into Settings — repo, read:org, read:user, user:email, and notifications. It's stored on your Mac and used to call api.github.com directly. There is no Beet server for it to travel to.

    Fine-grained tokens aren't supported yet — Beet checks the classic scope list, so a github_pat_ token reads as missing every scope.

  3. Let it watch

    Beet follows the repos you've pushed to in the last 30 days (7 or 90 if you prefer) plus any you pin — not every repo you can see. Mute the ones you don't care about and the list stays short.

Stop refreshing the PR page.
